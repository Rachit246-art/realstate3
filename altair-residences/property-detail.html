ARCHITECTURAL VISION

A Monolith of Light,
Water & Air.

Conceived by Altair's international design studio in collaboration with world-renowned Italian facade engineers, The Altair Heights stands as Mumbai's most exclusive vertical sanctuary. Each sky mansion occupies an entire half-floor or full-floor quadrant, ensuring private biometric elevator access and unhindered 270-degree Arabian Sea views.

The structural cantilever provides generous 14-foot ceiling voids, private 25m heated plunge pools on private sky-decks, and zero interior shear walls, granting total freedom for bespoke interior customization.
